How to play

Food

Hunger eats your life. Pick fruits, cook meat on a campfire, keep cans for later.

Warmth

Nights are cold. Stay near a campfire or wear warm clothes, a frozen devaster dies fast.

Radiation

Cities and toxic areas are irradiated. Get in, loot fast, get out, and drink a RadAway.

Stamina

Running empties your stamina. Your life comes back when you are fed, warm and clean.

Gather

Hit trees, stones and scrap with your tools. Better tools gather faster and unlock rarer materials.

Craft

Press C to craft. Stand next to a workbench or another machine to unlock its recipes.

Build

Walls, doors and floors keep the wasteland out. Repair them with a hammer and some nails.

Sleeping bag

Place one in your base: when you die, you wake up in it and keep your level.

Ghouls

At night, mutated ghouls hunt in the forest and attack bases. They run faster than you: hide behind your walls.

Devasters

Other players want your loot. Build a team to survive together, or fight alone if you dare.

Karma

Killing peaceful players lowers your karma and slows your progress. Peaceful devasters level up faster.

Traps

Landmines, spikes and automatic defenses guard some bases. Watch where you step.

Caps

A real death pays 1 cap every 1,000 score of the life, up to 500 caps. A sleeping bag rebirth pays nothing.

Ranks

All your lives add up to your lifetime score: 14 ranks from Wanderer to Devastator.

Leaderboards

Best life of the day, of the month and of all time in each game mode, by score, kills and survival time.

Your badge

Your rank badge shows next to your nickname in game. Your first nickname change is free.

Changelog

Version 0.36.3040 · Autumn update

Mirror, mirror, who's the prettiest devaster in the wasteland? The new Shop is open: pick a free color for your devaster in the Wardrobe, or spend your caps on crates and collect 44 new skins, from Common to Mythic. Want a wasteland of your own? Rent a private server and rule it with your moderators. The game also gets a new settings window with your own keys, a leaderboard for each mode, and a horde that lays siege to your base on every side.

  • Shop: the new button of the homepage, right under Clans, opens the Wardrobe, the Crates and the Lapa Cola stand.
  • 44 skins out of Russian folklore, the wasteland roads and the purge: Bludichka, Koschei, Baba Yaga, Chernobog, Skomorokh, Rusalka, Leshy, Magma, Uranium and many more. Five rarities, Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic, and everyone in the game sees the skin you wear.
  • Free colors: six new colors of the devaster for everyone, guests too: Rust, Acid, Frost, Smoke, Brick and Cobalt. Pick yours in the Wardrobe.
  • Crates: open them with your caps, they drop, shake and burst in the light of their best skin. The wood crate gives 1 skin for 500 caps, the steel crate 2 for 950, the hazard crate 3 for 1,400.
  • Airdrop: the rarest crate, 3 skins for 4,000 caps, and only skins you don't own yet.
  • Welcome caps: a new Devaster account starts with 500 caps instead of 100, enough for a first wood crate.
  • Lapa Cola stand: coming soon to the shop, packs of caps from 500 to 100,000. The bigger the pack, the more caps you get for a dollar.
  • Private servers: rent your own with the Private server button of the homepage, 1 month for $24.99 or 3 months for $49.99, in Frankfurt, Atlanta, Los Angeles, Osaka or Sydney. It's ready a few minutes after the payment, and your account is its owner: every command of the manual, no password. Renew it any time in My servers, the days add up. Lives played there earn no caps and don't count for your rank or the leaderboards.
  • Staff panel: run your private server in game with the star under the map button, or P. Name your moderators and tick what each one may do. Teleport, build, destroy and spawn with a click on the world or on the map. Ban an account or an address, keep the guests out or lock the server to your staff, and read the logs: who joined, who left, every command and who ran it.
  • New settings: the gear of the game opens a new window while the game goes on behind it. The keyboard, the resolution, the music, the sound effects and the particles as before, and the Controls: two keys for each action, yours to choose, kept on your device.
  • Kill my devaster: stuck somewhere? In the settings, stand still for 15 seconds and your devaster dies, like a death of hunger: your loot drops and your sleeping bag brings you back. A step or a blow stops it, and everyone around sees the red ring counting down.
  • Leaderboards by mode: Survival and Ghoul each get their own score, kills and time boards, Battle Royale soon. Lifetime still adds up every mode, like your rank. Scores above 45 million no longer show wrong on the in-game leaderboard.
  • Already playing: your survivor stayed on a server and you join another one? The game tells you where it plays, and Go back takes you to it: its life still counts.
  • Crafting pages: the craft and skill lists go by pages of 30 recipes, with arrows below them, so every recipe of a bench fits, however many you unlocked.
  • Uranium door: tougher, 12,500 life instead of 7,500, nearly as tough as the uranium wall.
  • Plasma turret: dearer, twice the materials of the laser turret, and a longer craft at the Tesla bench.
  • Ghoul mode: the horde starts smaller, 5 wild ghouls the first night, 15 the next, then 15 more each night, and gets faster night after night. It lays siege to your base on every side at once, walks around the trees and the rocks, and the ghouls with no room at a wall break the one next to it. Low walls and doors no longer hold them back, and a landmine goes off under a ghoul standing on it. At the end of a round, everyone goes to the score and joins the next one: no more frozen game.

Version 0.35.3026 · Turrets and chest

Did we really need more turrets? Here are two more: the sniper turret hits from farther than any other, and the plasma turret burns everything in its beam. Store more in the new metal chest, and wire your base with the new uranium cable wall and automatic door.

  • Sniper turret: shoots heavy bullets farther than any other turret, 100 damage a shot. But one shot every 1.3 seconds and a slow head: the ghouls that get close overrun it. Built from a turret base and a sniper at the research bench or the Tesla bench.
  • Plasma turret: 8 energy cells light a beam that burns every target in its line for 4 seconds, up to 306 damage, then it rests 2 seconds. The first wall, tree or stone stops the beam and burns too, never your team. Built from a turret base and a laser sniper at the Tesla bench.
  • Metal chest: 6 stacks instead of 4, still no food. Crafted at the research bench.
  • Uranium cable wall: connects to a cable like the cable wall, and irradiates anyone nearby. Crafted at the welding machine.
  • Uranium automatic door: opens and closes with a switch, and irradiates anyone nearby. Crafted at the welding machine.
  • Uranium wall: as tough as the metal wall, 15,000 life instead of 10,000.
  • Doors left open: the ghouls now break a low door left open, instead of hitting the air in front of it. Your Lapabots mend it too.
  • Zoom: the mouse wheel zooms out further, up to twice the default view.
  • Ghoul mode: harder, the horde grows by 7 wild ghouls each night instead of 5, still up to 80. The score no longer counts the XP 3 times, only 1.5 times: the levels still come 3 times faster.

Version 0.34.3016 · The horde

The nights of the Ghoul mode get crowded: a horde of wild ghouls now comes every night to help the ghouls finish off the last survivors. The map editor gets every building of the game and its logic, and the turrets are rebalanced.

  • Wild ghoul horde: in the Ghoul mode, once the time to build is over, wild ghouls come every night to help the ghoul players: 5 the first night, 5 more each night, up to 80. The ones you kill come back until the day burns them, and they leave the ghoul players alone.
  • They hunt you down: the horde tracks the nearest survivor across the whole map. It goes around the houses, but breaks through the buildings of your base to get you. On the ghoul drug, it doesn't hunt you.
  • Map editor: a new gear button holds every building that was missing: the workbenches, the machines, the fires, the sleeping bag, the turrets, the robots and the seeds.
  • Logic in the editor: press E on a switch, a timer or a lamp and walk on the platforms: the lamps light, the doors open and the cables glow where the signal runs. Try a circuit before you build it in game.
  • Turrets rebalanced: about 25% more materials to build them, and their heads turn 15% slower.
  • Camouflage gear: while you wear it, a turret only spots you within 60% of its range.
  • Boars and deer: they roam the whole map, around the trees, the rocks and the buildings, with a calmer walk: only their legs move.
  • Clans: a clan name can be as short as 2 letters or digits.

Version 0.34.3015 · Towers

Your base can now defend itself. Build a rifle turret or a laser turret, feed it the ammo of its gun, and it shoots the ghouls, the devasters, or both, even while you are away. And your rank now raises how much you can build, while living boars and deer roam the map.

  • Rifle turret: shoots heavy bullets, the ammo of the AK47. Built from a turret base, made of shaped metal and electronics, and an AK47: both at the research bench or the Tesla bench.
  • Laser turret: shoots energy cells, farther and harder. Built from a turret base and a laser submachine at the Tesla bench.
  • Its window: open it like a campfire. The + loads 30 rounds, 255 at most, and a click on the rounds takes them back. Switch the ghouls and the devasters on or off, together or apart. Only you and your team can open your turrets.
  • Read it at a glance: yellow lights on standby, a green ring while it shoots, red when it is out of ammo. Empty, it stops shooting and its ring flashes red while an enemy is in range.
  • Building limit by rank: a guest builds up to 950 buildings, each rank from Settler adds 150, up to 2,450 for a Devastator. Point at a rank in your account to see what it gives.
  • Living boars: they roam the map day and night, and the ghouls leave them alone. Hit one and it charges you back for a few seconds. Killed, it leaves its carcass to harvest with an axe.
  • Living deer: they bolt as soon as you get close, so sneak up or take a bow. Killed, they leave their carcass to harvest with an axe.
  • Your survivor waits for you: logged in, after 10 minutes of play, your survivor stays in the world when you leave, until you come back, even from another device. The ghouls and the other devasters can still find it.
  • Servers keep their world: after a restart, the survivors and their bases come back.
  • Clans: 50 members at most, and the ten blazons redrawn.
  • Rank insignia show on the in-game leaderboard.

Version 0.33.3000 · Devaster accounts and clans

The wasteland finally remembers you. Create your Devaster account with an email and a password, right above the changelog, and every life you play counts. Then gather your devasters under one clan and fight for the top of the weekly clan leaderboard.

  • Lapa Cola caps: 100 caps to welcome you, then 1 cap every 1,000 score of each life, up to 500 caps per life.
  • 14 ranks: all your lives add up to your lifetime score, from Wanderer to Devastator. Your rank badge shows before your name in game, for everyone to see.
  • Leaderboards: best life of the day, of the month and of all time, by score, kills and survival time, plus the lifetime ranking. Find your own position at the bottom.
  • Your profile: lifetime score, kills, deaths, kill / death ratio, best score, best kills, longest life, time played, your 5 best lives and how far the next rank is.
  • Your nickname comes with a #tag, so two devasters can wear the same name. The first change is free, then it costs 100 caps.
  • Clans: found one for 150 caps, joining and leaving are free. The [NAME] of your clan shows in its color before your nickname, next to your team tag.
  • Weekly clan leaderboard: when a member dies, the score of the life goes to the clan. Every Monday at 00:00 UTC, the 1st clan wins uranium, alloys and junk, the 2nd alloys and junk, the 3rd junk, in its inventory.
  • Clan shop: the leader spends the inventory, 1 junk to change the clan color, in game too, or 1 alloys to raise one of 10 blazons.
  • Find a clan: search all the clans and ask up to 5 of them. The leader accepts or refuses, can remove a member and hands over the lead before leaving.
  • Forgot your password? A reset link comes by email. Changing your password logs out your other devices.
  • Fair play: cheaters can be banned for a while or for good. A banned account can't play with its account and leaves the leaderboards until the ban ends, the countdown shows when you log in.
  • A new homepage: the account panel, this changelog, the Clans window and a How to play guide with Lapabot, in the colors of the machine.

Guests can still play as before: log in only if you want your lives to count.
